About Mentor's Plus

Loyola Heights, Quezon City — reviewing students for entrance exams since 2018.

Our story

Mentor's Plus started with two tutors and a whiteboard in a rented classroom along Katipunan, working with a handful of students preparing for UPCAT. What made the difference wasn't more hours of review — it was tutors who understood exactly how each exam is built and scored. Word spread, the classroom filled up, and the tutoring roster grew to cover ACET, DCAT, and USTET as well.

Today, Mentor's Plus runs 1-on-1 tutoring, classroom review, and online sessions for hundreds of students each term, still from the same neighborhood where it started.

Mission

To give every reviewee a clear, honest picture of where they stand — and a plan to close the gap before exam day.

Vision

To be the entrance exam review center Quezon City students recommend to their younger siblings.

Our team

35+ tutors across Math, Science, English, and Filipino.
